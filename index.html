Explore the Club

Use the interactive floor plans to explore member-facing facilities throughout Tokyo American Club.

  1. Select a floor from the Floor Selector.
  2. Use category filters or search for a facility.
  3. Click a colored marker to open facility information.
  4. Drag the map to pan and use the zoom controls as needed.
  5. Select a category in the top navigation to browse all facilities in that category.
